# Bayes' Theorem Study Notes

## Prior
- **Definition**: The initial probability distribution reflecting our knowledge about possible values of the variable before observing new information. [00:17:14–00:17:43]

## Likelihood
- **Definition**: The probability of observing the data given the variable of interest. [00:17:14–00:17:43]

## Posterior
- **Definition**: The updated probability distribution after incorporating new observations. Formally: `posterior = likelihood × prior / evidence` [00:16:01–00:16:45]

## Evidence
- **Definition**: The normalization constant that ensures the posterior is a valid probability distribution (integrates to 1). [00:16:45–00:17:14]

## Conceptual Explanation
- Bayes' theorem converts prior knowledge into posterior knowledge by updating with new observations. [00:17:14–00:17:43]

## Key Insight
- It solves inverse probabilistic problems: given observed data, infer the variable of interest. [00:17:14–00:17:43]
