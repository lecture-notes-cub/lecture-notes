# Rare Disease Test Calculation

## Key Definitions
- **True Positive Rate (Sensitivity)**: Probability of testing positive given disease presence
- **False Positive Rate**: Probability of testing positive given disease absence
- **Disease Prevalence**: Proportion of population with disease
- **Positive Predictive Value (PPV)**: Probability of having disease given positive test

### Critical Insight

## Why This Happens
- Low disease prevalence means most positive tests are false positives
- Example: If 1% of population has disease and test is 99% accurate, 99% of people without disease test positive
- Result: Only 1% of positive tests are true positives
