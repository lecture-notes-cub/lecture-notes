## Gamma-Poisson Bayesian Update (Corrected Final Derivation)

### Key Definitions
- **Poisson Distribution**: $P(X_i = x_i | \lambda) = \frac{\lambda^{x_i} e^{-\lambda}}{x_i!}$
- **Gamma Prior**: $p(\lambda) = \frac{\lambda^{a-1} e^{-\lambda/b}}{\Gamma(a)}$ (shape $a$, rate $b$)
- **Joint Likelihood**: $P(X_1, ..., X_n | \lambda) = \prod_{i=1}^n \frac{\lambda^{x_i} e^{-\lambda}}{x_i!}$

### Derivation Steps
1. **Joint Posterior**: 
   $p(\lambda | X_1, ..., X_n) \propto \left( \prod_{i=1}^n \frac{\lambda^{x_i} e^{-\lambda}}{x_i!} \right) \cdot \frac{\lambda^{a-1} e^{-\lambda/b}}{\Gamma(a)}$

2. **Simplify**: 
   - Combine exponents: $e^{-\lambda(n + 1/b)}$
   - Combine powers: $\lambda^{\sum x_i + a - 1}$
   - Constants: $\frac{1}{\prod x_i! \Gamma(a)}$

3. **Posterior Distribution**: 
   $p(\lambda | X_1, ..., X_n) = \frac{\lambda^{(\sum x_i + a) - 1} e^{-\lambda(n + b)}}{\Gamma(\sum x_i + a)}$ 
   (shape $a + \sum x_i$, rate $b + n$)

### Why This Works
- **Conjugate Prior**: Gamma distribution is conjugate to Poisson likelihood.
- **Posterior is Gamma**: After updating, the posterior distribution remains Gamma with updated parameters.
