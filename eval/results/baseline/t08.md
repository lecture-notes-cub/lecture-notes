## Asymptotic Properties of Maximum-Likelihood Estimators

### [00:22:03–00:22:53] Introduction to MLE Properties
- Maximum likelihood estimate (MLE) is a deterministic function of observations $X_1, X_n$
- MLE is a random variable due to random observations
- **Key claim**: Expectation of MLE is asymptotically unbiased (i.e., $\theta_{ml} \to \theta$ as $n \to \infty$)

### [00:22:53–00:23:38] Asymptotic Unbiasedness
- MLE oscillates around true parameter value $\theta$ with decreasing amplitude as $n$ increases
- **Definition**: Asymptotic unbiasedness means $E[\theta_{ml}] \to \theta$ for large $n$ (not exact unbiasedness)
- *Note: Unclear phrasing* - 'this is a very good product' likely refers to the property being useful

### [00:23:39–00:24:25] Consistency
- **Definition**: Consistency means $\theta_{ml} \to \theta$ as $n \to \infty$ (probability 1)
- *Correction*: Speaker initially misstates the term as 'asymptotic and biasness' (likely meant 'asymptotic unbiasedness')
- *Note*: Speaker clarifies this is the property of consistency

### [00:24:25–00:25:07] Convergence to True Value
- For large $n$, MLE converges to true parameter value $\theta$ (consistency)
- *Note*: Speaker mentions this was proven in statistics courses

### [00:25:07–00:25:41] Asymptotic Normality
- MLE has approximately normal distribution for large $n$:
  $$\theta_{ml} \sim N(\theta, \frac{1}{n}I(\theta)^{-1})$$
  where $I(\theta)$ is the Fisher information matrix
- **Variance behavior**: $\text{Var}(\theta_{ml}) \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$ (supports consistency)

### [00:25:41–00:26:16] Variance Scaling
- Variance of MLE scales as $\frac{1}{n}$ with sample size
- *Example*: If $n$ increases by 10x, variance decreases by 10x
- *Note*: Speaker uses $\text{\text{f}}$ (likely typo for $\phi$) for Fisher information

### [00:26:23–00:26:58] Cramér-Rao Lower Bound
- **Cramér-Rao inequality**: Among all unbiased estimators, MLE achieves the minimum possible variance
- **Lower bound**: $\text{Var}(\hat{\theta}) \geq \frac{1}{nI(\theta)}$ (for unbiased estimators)
- *Note*: Speaker mentions this is related to Heisenberg uncertainty principle

### [00:26:58–00:27:28] Efficiency
- MLE achieves the Cramér-Rao lower bound (minimum variance)
- **Definition**: Efficient estimator has smallest possible variance among unbiased estimators
- *Note*: Speaker states MLE is optimal for unknown parameter

### [00:27:28–00:27:59] Summary of Key Properties
- **Asymptotic unbiasedness**: $E[\theta_{ml}] \to \theta$ as $n \to \infty$
- **Consistency**: $\theta_{ml} \to \theta$ as $n \to \infty$ (probability 1)
- **Asymptotic normality**: $\theta_{ml} \sim N(\theta, \frac{1}{n}I(\theta)^{-1})$ for large $n$
- **Efficiency**: MLE achieves Cramér-Rao lower bound (minimum variance)
