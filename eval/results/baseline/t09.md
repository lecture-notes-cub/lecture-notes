## Two-Heads Coin Example: Small-Sample MLE Limitations

### [00:27:59–00:28:37] Estimation in Statistics
- Estimation is popular in statistics and machine learning
- **Key limitation**: Properties of estimators hold only when $n$ is much larger than 1 (or $n \to \infty$)
- **Problem**: Finite samples often lack sufficient observations to ensure these properties

- Observed: $n$ tosses, $k$ heads
- **MLE intuition**: Frequency of heads ($\hat{p} = k/n$)

- **Example**: 2 tosses, 2 heads
- **MLE result**: $\hat{p} = 1$ (probability of heads)

- **Contrast**: With $n=1000$ tosses, MLE $\hat{p} = k/1000$ likely accurate

- **Drawback**: MLE can produce results contradicting common sense when $n$ is small
- **Critical point**: Small samples ($n \ll \infty$) violate assumptions for MLE validity
