- The speaker confirms they performed Bayesian inference correctly but made a *model specification error*.
- **Why the model was optimistic**: The model assumed *P(T | E)* was constant, but reality shows *P(T | E) > P(T | ¬E)*.
- **Consequence**: The low posterior probability (~2%) was *correct* under the model but *inadequate* for real-world conditions.
