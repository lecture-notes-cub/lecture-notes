# Conditional Probability and Independence

## Definition of Conditional Probability

The conditional distribution of $X$ given $Y$ is defined as:

$$P(X | Y) = \frac{P(X, Y)}{P(Y)}$$

This represents how the distribution of $X$ changes when we observe $Y$.

## Independence

Two random variables $X$ and $Y$ are independent if:

$$P(X, Y) = P(X) \cdot P(Y)$$

When $X$ and $Y$ are independent, the conditional distribution simplifies to:

$$P(X | Y) = P(X)$$

This means observing $Y$ provides no information about $X$.

## Key Insight

- If $X$ and $Y$ are independent, then $Y$ contains no information about $X$.
- If $X$ and $Y$ are dependent, observing $Y$ changes the distribution of $X$.
