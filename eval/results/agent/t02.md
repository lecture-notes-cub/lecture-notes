# Variance Interpretation: Observing Y Reduces Variance of X

## Key Definitions
- **Unconditional Variance of X** (`Var(X)`): Total variability of X without considering Y
- **Conditional Variance of X given Y** (`Var(X|Y)`): Variance of X when Y is known (depends on Y)

## Core Reasoning
- `Var(X) ≥ E[Var(X|Y)]` (unconditional variance is always ≥ conditional variance averaged over Y)
- Observing Y provides information that reduces the variance of X when X and Y are interdependent

## Example
- If X and Y are correlated, knowing Y allows us to predict X more precisely, reducing uncertainty (variance)
