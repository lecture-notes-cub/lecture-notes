## Conceptual Explanation

Bayes' theorem shows how we can convert knowledge about a variable of interest (y) using observed information. We start with the prior distribution (our initial knowledge about y), then incorporate observations (expressed via the likelihood function) to update our knowledge to the posterior distribution.

The formula is: 

$$
\text{Posterior} = \frac{\text{Likelihood} \times \text{Prior}}{\text{Evidence}}
$$

Where the evidence is the normalization constant that ensures the posterior is a valid probability distribution (integrates to 1).

## Physical Sense

This is a way of solving inverse probabilistic problems. We update our knowledge about y based on observations related to y. The theorem transforms prior knowledge into posterior knowledge through the likelihood function.
