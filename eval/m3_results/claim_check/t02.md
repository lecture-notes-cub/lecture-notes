# Variance Interpretation When Observing Another Variable

**Key Definition**
- Unconditional variance of X: Total variability of X without considering Y
- Conditional variance of X given Y: Variability of X that remains after accounting for Y

**Core Relationship**
- \( \text{Var}(X) \geq \text{Var}(X|Y) \) with equality iff Y is independent of X
  - *Citation*: [00:05:31–00:06:07]

**Interpretation**
- Observing Y provides information that reduces X's variance when X and Y are interdependent
  - *Citation*: [00:06:07–00:06:41]

**Why This Matters**
- This inequality is fundamental for understanding how observations reduce uncertainty in dependent variables
- Proving it is useful despite seeming obvious
