# Frequentist vs Bayesian Interpretation of Randomness

## Key Distinction

- **Frequentist view**: Randomness is *objective* and *unpredictable by definition*. A random variable is a process whose outcome is inherently unpredictable.
  - *Example*: Tossing a coin (theoretical unpredictability)
  - *Timestamp*: [00:34:56–00:35:40]

- **Bayesian view**: Randomness is *subjective ignorance*. A random variable is the result of a deterministic process whose outcome is unknown due to missing information.
  - *Example*: Train arrival time (predictable with full information, uncertain with partial information)
  - *Timestamp*: [00:35:40–00:36:16]
