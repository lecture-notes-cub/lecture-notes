# Product Rule and Chain Rule

## Definition
The joint distribution of random variables can be decomposed into a product of conditional distributions. For two variables:

$$P(X, Y) = P(Y | X)P(X) = P(X | Y)P(Y)$$

This is the **product rule** (sometimes called the chain rule in probability).

## Alternative Variable Orderings
For $n$ variables, the joint distribution can be decomposed in multiple ways:

- **Forward order**: $P(X_1, X_2, ..., X_n) = P(X_1 | X_2, ..., X_n)P(X_2 | X_3, ..., X_n) \cdots P(X_n)$
- **Reverse order**: $P(X_n | X_1, ..., X_{n-1})P(X_{n-1} | X_1, ..., X_{n-2}) \cdots P(X_1)$
