- **Real-world mechanism**: Thieves listen to the radio and interpret small earthquakes as *less likely* to be caused by theft, increasing their activity.
- **Example**: If a small earthquake is announced, thieves may assume it’s a natural event (not a break-in), leading to *higher* burglary rates.
