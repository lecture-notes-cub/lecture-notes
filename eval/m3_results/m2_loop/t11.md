# Frequentist vs Bayesian Interpretation of Randomness

## Key Definitions

- **Frequentist View** (classical view):
  - Random variable: Process whose outcome is *unpredictable by definition*
  - Uncertainty is *objective* (inherent in the process)
  - Example: Coin toss (theoretical unpredictability)

- **Bayesian View**:
  - Random variable: Result of a *completely deterministic process* whose outcome is unknown due to *subjective ignorance*
  - Uncertainty is *subjective* (lack of information)
  - Example: Train arrival time (predictable with more information)

## Core Distinction

The fundamental difference lies in the nature of uncertainty:
- **Frequentist**: Uncertainty is *inherent* to the process (e.g., coin toss)
- **Bayesian**: Uncertainty is *informational* (e.g., train arrival time with incomplete knowledge)
