# Asymptotic Properties of Maximum-Likelihood Estimators

## Consistency

- **Definition**: As $n \to \infty$, the MLE $\hat{\theta}_{\text{ml}}$ converges in probability to the true parameter $\theta$.
- **Timestamp**: [00:24:25–00:25:07]
- **Key Reasoning**: The expectation of $\hat{\theta}_{\text{ml}}$ is asymptotically unbiased (i.e., $E[\hat{\theta}_{\text{ml}}] \to \theta$ as $n \to \infty$), and the variance of $\hat{\theta}_{\text{ml}}$ approaches zero.

## Asymptotic Normality

- **Definition**: For large $n$, $\sqrt{n}(\hat{\theta}_{\text{ml}} - \theta) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, I^{-1}(\theta))$, where $I(\theta)$ is the Fisher information matrix.
- **Timestamp**: [00:25:07–00:26:16]
- **Key Reasoning**: The MLE has a normal distribution with mean $\theta$ and variance $\frac{1}{nI(\theta)}$.

## Efficiency

- **Definition**: The MLE achieves the Cramér-Rao lower bound for variance among all unbiased estimators.
- **Timestamp**: [00:26:23–00:27:28]
- **Key Reasoning**: The Cramér-Rao bound states that $\text{Var}(\hat{\theta}) \geq \frac{1}{nI(\theta)}$, and the MLE attains this bound.

## Summary of Asymptotic Properties

| Property | Condition | Key Result |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| Consistency | $n \to \infty$ | $\hat{\theta}_{\text{ml}} \to \theta$ in probability |
| Asymptotic Normality | Large $n$ | $\sqrt{n}(\hat{\theta}_{\text{ml}} - \theta) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, I^{-1}(\theta))$ |
| Efficiency | Unbiased estimators | $\text{Var}(\hat{\theta}_{\text{ml}}) = \frac{1}{nI(\theta)}$ (Cramér-Rao lower bound) |
