# Variance Interpretation: Observing Another Variable

## Key Definitions
- **Unconditional Variance of X**: Total variability of X without considering Y
- **Conditional Variance of X given Y**: Variability of X that remains after accounting for Y (varies with Y)

## Core Reasoning
- The unconditional variance of X is **always ≥** the conditional variance of X given Y
  - *Citation*: [00:05:31–00:06:07]
- Observing Y reduces the variance of X when X and Y are interdependent
  - *Citation*: [00:05:31–00:06:41]

## Example
- If X and Y are interdependent (e.g., X = height, Y = age), observing Y provides information to reduce uncertainty about X
