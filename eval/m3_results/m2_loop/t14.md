# Large-Data Limit and Practical Advantages of Bayesian Methods

## Large-Data Limit

The lecture explains that in the limit when the number of observations $n$ is much larger than the number of parameters $d$ (i.e., $n \gg d$), the posterior distribution in Bayesian inference converges to a delta function around the maximum likelihood estimate (MLE). This means that for large datasets, Bayesian methods and classical statistics (specifically MLE) become equivalent.

**Key point**: When $n \gg d$, Bayesian posterior $\rightarrow \delta$ (MLE)
