# Variance Interpretation When Observing Another Variable

## Key Definitions
- **Unconditional Variance of X** (total variability of X without considering Y)
- **Conditional Variance of X given Y** (variability of X that remains after accounting for Y, which depends on Y)

## Core Relationship
The unconditional variance of X is always greater than or equal to the conditional expectation of the conditional variance of X given Y:

$$\text{Var}(X) \geq \mathbb{E}[\text{Var}(X|Y)]$$

This inequality holds because the conditional variance depends on Y, and averaging over Y reduces the overall variance.

## Interpretation
Observing Y provides information that reduces the variance of X when X and Y are interdependent. This is a fundamental property of conditional variance.
