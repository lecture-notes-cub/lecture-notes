## Bayes' Theorem Equation

The theorem is expressed as:

$$
\mathbb{P}(y|x) = \frac{\mathbb{P}(x|y) \cdot \mathbb{P}(y)}{\mathbb{P}(x)}
$$

Where:
- $\mathbb{P}(y|x)$: Posterior
- $\mathbb{P}(x|y)$: Likelihood
- $\mathbb{P}(y)$: Prior
- $\mathbb{P}(x)$: Evidence

## Conceptual Explanation

[00:17:14–00:17:43] *'Bayes' theorem shows how we can convert our knowledge about variable of interest. We start from prior distribution that reflects our prior information about possible values of y. Then we take into account observations that are expressed in terms of this likelihood function. And applying Bayesian theorem, we transform our prior knowledge to posterior knowledge. So we update our knowledge about the value of y.'*
