## Marginalization and Expectation Interpretation

### Key Definitions and Reasoning

- **Marginalization**: The process of integrating out (or summing over) a variable to obtain the unconditional distribution of another variable.

- **Expectation Interpretation**: The marginal distribution of $x$ can be interpreted as the expectation of the conditional distribution $P(x|y)$ with respect to $y$.
