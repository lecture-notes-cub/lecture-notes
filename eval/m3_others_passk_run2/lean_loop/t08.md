# Asymptotic Properties of Maximum-Likelihood Estimators

## Asymptotic Unbiasedness
- **Definition**: As $n \to \infty$, $\hat{\theta}_{\text{ML}}$ converges in expectation to $\theta$ (true parameter).
  - **Citation**: [00:22:53–00:23:38]
- **Reasoning**: The MLE is a random variable that oscillates around $\theta$ with decreasing amplitude as $n$ increases.

## Consistency
- **Definition**: $\lim_{n \to \infty} \hat{\theta}_{\text{ML}} = \theta$ almost surely.
  - **Citation**: [00:24:25–00:25:07]
- **Key Insight**: This follows from asymptotic unbiasedness and the variance of $\hat{\theta}_{\text{ML}}$ decreasing to zero.

## Asymptotic Normality
- **Definition**: For large $n$, $\sqrt{n}(\hat{\theta}_{\text{ML}} - \theta) \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}(0, I^{-1}(\theta))$, where $I(\theta)$ is the Fisher information matrix.
  - **Citation**: [00:25:07–00:26:16]
- **Variance**: $\text{Var}(\hat{\theta}_{\text{ML}}) \approx \frac{1}{n} I^{-1}(\theta)$

## Efficiency (Cramér-Rao Lower Bound)
- **Definition**: The MLE achieves the Cramér-Rao lower bound for variance among unbiased estimators.
  - **Citation**: [00:26:23–00:27:59]
- **Key Insight**: The variance of $\hat{\theta}_{\text{ML}}$ is $\frac{1}{n} I^{-1}(\theta)$, which is the minimum possible variance for unbiased estimators.
